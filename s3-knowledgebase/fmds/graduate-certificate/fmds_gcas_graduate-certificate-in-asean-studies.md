## Program Description

The **Graduate Certificate in ASEAN Studies (GCAS)** is part of a broader ASEAN Studies Graduate Program that also includes a Master's Degree. The framework of the program was jointly developed by five Open Universities in the ASEAN region: **Universitas Terbuka** (Indonesia), **Open University of Malaysia**, **Sukhothai Thammathirat Open University** (Thailand), **Hanoi Open University** (Vietnam), and the **UP Open University** (Philippines).

The program is intended to promote regional and global understanding about ASEAN countries from the perspective of ASEAN itself; to increase the ASEAN community's consciousness of its common historical, cultural, and regional identity; and to obtain a balanced perspective in ASEAN studies.

It is designed for post-baccalaureate individuals, professionals, and practitioners — including diplomats, policy analysts, educators, business people, public servants, NGO staff, and development workers — who are interested, qualified, and academically competent to pursue graduate-level education in ASEAN studies.

---

## Program Goals

The Graduate Certificate program aims to develop graduates who are able to:

1. Demonstrate comprehensive understanding, insights, and mastery about ASEAN and the countries comprising it.
2. Contribute to an enhanced and heightened ASEAN community's consciousness and awareness of its ties of history, culture, and its common regional identity while celebrating diversity.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught **online** following the principles of **guided independent study** and **collaborative inquiry**, consistent with UPOU's open and distance learning framework.

## Curriculum

The Graduate Certificate in ASEAN Studies consists of **15 units** of course work:

| Course Category | Units |
|---|---|
| Major / Core Courses | 9 units |
| Elective Courses | 6 units |
| **Total** | **15 units** |

> A student who completes the Graduate Certificate may opt to graduate with the degree and seek admission into the Master's program within **three years** of earning the Graduate Certificate, provided they have a **GWA of 2.0 or better**.

## Courses and Program of Study

### Major Courses — Required (9 units)

| Course Code | Course Title | Course Description | Units |
|---|---|---|---|
| ASEAN 201 | ASEAN Studies I | Introduction to the field of ASEAN studies: the region's geography, cultures, history, politics, economies, record of interactions, and selected current issues facing the region. | 3 units |
| ASEAN 202 | ASEAN Studies II | ASEAN regionalism, regional order, regional integration, and community building through various theoretical perspectives. | 3 units |
| ASEAN 203 | The ASEAN Organization | The ASEAN as an organic entity established by the individual and collective will of the member organizations; history, organizational, structural, and functional changes and developments, and the influence of intra-ASEAN and extra-ASEAN factors on the organization's evolution. | 3 units |
