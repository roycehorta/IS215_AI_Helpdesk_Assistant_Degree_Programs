# Diploma in Social Work

## Program Description

The Diploma in Social Work (DSW) program is designed for professionals engaged in social welfare and social development work who wish to pursue graduate studies to gain a higher level of competence in their field of practice. The program is open to those with a bachelor's degree in social work (BSSW) and those without, with a different track for each. An above-average performance or the successful completion of the Diploma qualifies students without a BSSW to enter the Master of Social Work program should they wish to continue their studies.

## Program Goals

The Diploma in Social Work aims to provide higher level competence in direct practice and supervisory tasks among social work practitioners.

## Mode of Instruction

All courses, except for the field instruction courses, are delivered mainly by distance education mode. Field instruction courses, an integral part of social work education, require face-to-face supervision accomplished with the support of agency field work supervisors working under the overall direction of the faculty-in-charge.

Prospective applicants are advised that the program requires two field instruction courses for BSSW holders (320 hours each in an assigned agency). Students without a BSSW who intend to pursue a Master of Social Work and complete the 1,000-hour requirement for professional social work training will be required to enrol in a total of five field instruction courses.

Students whose employment does not allow adjustment of their work schedule may apply for a work-study scheme to conduct field instruction in their own agency, subject to approval.

## Curriculum

- **With BSSW:** 27 units (15 core + 6 specialization + 6 field instruction and electives)
- **Without BSSW:** 33 units (15 core + 12 specialization/survey + 6 field instruction)

The program can be completed in five to six semesters on a part-time basis at six units per semester. Students must pass a comprehensive examination to earn the degree.
