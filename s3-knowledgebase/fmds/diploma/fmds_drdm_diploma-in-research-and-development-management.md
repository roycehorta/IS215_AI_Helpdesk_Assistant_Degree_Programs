# Diploma in Research and Development Management

**Institution:** University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU)  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Management and Development Studies  
**Level:** Graduate (Diploma)  
**Source:** https://fmds.upou.edu.ph/academics/post-bac/drdm/

---

## Program Description

The Diploma in Research and Development (R&D) Management is a post-baccalaureate program directed toward managerial leadership development in organizations engaged in R&D and technology development. It is ladderized to the Master of Research and Development Management (MR&DM).

---

## Program Goals

* Demonstrate enhanced managerial leadership with an R&D-oriented perspective.
* Influence and transform R&D organizations to be productive, dynamic, and socially relevant.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught online following the principles of guided independent study and collaborative inquiry.

---

The program consists of 21 units of coursework.
