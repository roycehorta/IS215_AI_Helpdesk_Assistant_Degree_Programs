# Diploma in Land Valuation and Management

**Institution:** University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU)  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Management and Development Studies  
**Level:** Graduate (Diploma)  
**Source:** https://fmds.upou.edu.ph/academics/post-bac/dlvm/

---

## Program Description

The Diploma in Land Valuation and Management (DLVM) program addresses the need for high-quality valuation education accessible to private and government valuers. It caters to those already in the field and those wishing to enter the profession. It is ladderized to the Master of Land Valuation and Management (MLVM).

---

## Program Goals

* Develop professional valuers and property managers whose standards are globally competitive.
* Promote the growth of the real estate industry while contributing to sustainable development.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught online following the principles of guided independent study and collaborative inquiry.

---

The program requires 24 units of coursework.
