# Diploma in Land Use Planning

**Institution:** University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU)  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Management and Development Studies  
**Level:** Graduate (Diploma)  
**Source:** https://fmds.upou.edu.ph/academics/post-bac/dlup/

---

## Program Description

The Diploma in Land Use Planning (DLUP) caters to local government planners and those in municipal, city, and provincial levels who handle planning responsibilities under the Local Government Code of 1991. Graduates may be eligible for the Environmental Planners Licensure Examination.

---

## Program Goals

* Provide access to planning education for personnel of LGUs and government agencies.
* Decentralize planning education capability outside Metro Manila.
* Strengthen training institutions' capacity at the regional level to support distance education in land use planning.

---

## Mode of Instruction

Primarily delivered online. The course on Urban Planning Workshop may involve face-to-face meetings.

---

## Curriculum

The Diploma consists of 21 units of core and major courses, typically completed within three to four semesters.
