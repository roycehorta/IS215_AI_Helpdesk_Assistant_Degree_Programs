# Diploma in International Health

**Institution:** University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU)  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Management and Development Studies  
**Level:** Graduate (Diploma)  
**Source:** https://fmds.upou.edu.ph/academics/post-bac/dih/

---

## Program Description

International health focuses on public health across regional or national boundaries. The DIH program seeks to understand health problems in a global context, compare factors affecting health in various economies, and develop means of disease reduction and health protection. It is ladderized to the Master of International Health (MIH).

---

## Program Goals

* Design health programs addressing global health problems (e.g., pandemics, disasters).
* Implement services in a global context for health promotion and development.
* Evaluate health development programs and strategies across different settings.
* Engage in effective collaboration with international and non-government organizations.
