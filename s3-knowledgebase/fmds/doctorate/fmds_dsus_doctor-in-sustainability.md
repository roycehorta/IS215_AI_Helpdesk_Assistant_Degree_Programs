# Doctor in Sustainability

## Program Description

The Doctor in Sustainability (DSus) is a professional doctorate program that aims to produce graduates who can develop research-based solutions to persistent sustainability challenges. It addresses the need for professionals with advanced academic training who are expected to develop transformative research approaches to address wicked problems based on practical issues in their profession.

The DSus marries theoretical frameworks from the academic community with practical knowledge and experiences drawn from the field and work-based settings. It provides enough flexibility to strengthen students' proficiency in the pillars of sustainability — economic, social, and environmental. By providing a shared learning environment for practitioners and academics working on sustainability, the program allows for co-design and co-creation of knowledge through coursework and dissertation.

The program is designed to produce professional doctorates who are: analyst, facilitator, communicator, integrator, negotiator, and translator of pluridisciplinary perspectives. It accommodates different disciplines, philosophies of science and methodologies, as long as these lead to innovative solutions that contribute to transitions towards sustainability.

## Program Goals

The Doctor in Sustainability program aims to develop graduates who are able to:

1. Demonstrate highly advanced systematic knowledge and skills in highly specialized and/or complex interdisciplinary or multidisciplinary fields of learning;
2. Utilize complex research/creative work and/or professional practice with full independence in individual work and/or in interdisciplinary or multidisciplinary teams;
3. Apply a complex setting that demands leadership in research and creative work with strategic value added;
4. Apply a significant level of expertise-based autonomy and accountability to professional leadership for innovation, research, and/or development management;
5. Evaluate the complexity of sustainability issues and challenges in institutional, local, national, regional and global settings;
6. Design pluridisciplinary, intersectoral, and intergenerational equity-informed sustainability frameworks, models and systems;
7. Generate transformative, innovative and context-based solutions in addressing complex global societal and environmental challenges; and
8. Demonstrate advanced leadership in professional sustainability research settings through management of pluridisciplinary teams and collaborative partnerships.

## Curriculum

**Total Units: 46**

- Core Courses: 15 units
- Elective Courses: 12 units
- Research Courses: 7 units
- Doctoral Dissertation: 12 units

*One (1) publication in a peer-reviewed journal is also a requirement for graduation.*

### Elective Courses (12 units — choose 4)

Electives are drawn from a list of eligible courses from UPOU or other CUs, guided by the research panel/advisory committee. Students should choose electives that strengthen their capacity to pursue sustainability research across the three pillars: environmental, socio-political, and economic.
