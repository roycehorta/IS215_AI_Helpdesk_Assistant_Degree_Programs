# Master of Public Management

## Program Description

The Master of Public Management (MPM) Program of the Faculty of Management and Development Studies, UP Open University (UPOU) was first offered during the second semester of AY 1997-1998. The program was initiated in collaboration with the UP National College of Public Administration and Governance (NCPAG) with support from the Civil Service Commission, aiming to democratize education by offering programs in distance mode.

The Program currently offers three specializations: Local Government and Regional Administration (LGRA), Public Policy and Program Administration (PPPA), and Voluntary Sector Management (VSM).

## Program Goals

The MPM Program is designed for policy-makers, administrators, and managers of public, private, and non-governmental organizations; practitioners in local government and administration; and individuals interested in good governance, public policy and administration.

The program aims to:

- Provide a high level of competence in public policy, governance, and administration;
- Prepare students to become better public managers, administrators, government or volunteer workers and/or advocates of social change, administrative reform, and national development; and
- Equip students with skills and knowledge needed in managing and developing organizations that work for the welfare and interest of the Filipino people.

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are delivered in Distance Education (DE) mode using multimedia technology. Teachers and students interact via the internet mainly through the MOODLE platform accessible at MyPortal LMS.

## Curriculum

The MPM Program consists of 36 units of academic course work and a written Comprehensive Examination. The 36 units include: five mandatory core courses (15 units), two survey courses (6 units), three courses in the field of specialization (9 units), and two electives (6 units).

## Program of Study

A Graduate Admission Test (GAT) is required before admission to the MPM Program.

| Term | Courses | Units |
|------|---------|-------|
| 1st Semester | 1 Core + 1 Survey | 6 |
| 2nd Semester | 2 Core | 6 |
| 3rd Semester | 1 Core + 1 Major | 6 |
| 4th Semester | 1 Major + 1 Survey | 6 |
| 5th Semester | 1 Core + 1 Major | 6 |
| 6th Semester | 1 Major/Survey + 1 Elective | 6 |
| **Total** | | **36 units** |

*Comprehensive Examination required after completing 36 units with a GWA of 2.0 or better. Students have two chances to pass both the general field and area of specialization exams.*
