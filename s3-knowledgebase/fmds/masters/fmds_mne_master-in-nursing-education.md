# Master in Nursing Education

## Program Description

The Master in Nursing Education (MNE) program is a 30-unit professional graduate program that prepares nurses to assume the roles of teacher, good clinician, and leader manager in higher education institutions. The program is jointly offered by UP Manila (UPM) College of Nursing and UPOU Faculty of Management and Development Studies (FMDS), delivered in a combination of face-to-face and online modes.

The educator role demonstrated in this program encompasses diverse skills: curriculum development, clinical teaching and supervision, classroom teaching, laboratory and practicum teaching, application of knowledge of the learning process, and management of the learning environment. The program emphasizes professionalism, lifelong learning, collaboration, and communication.

## Program Goals

At the end of the program, graduates can:

1. Perform skills relevant to curriculum design, implementation, monitoring, evaluation, and management based on contemporary educational theories, principles, and models;
2. Facilitate learning through the application of theoretical concepts among diverse learners with different learning needs and styles;
3. Maintain advanced knowledge and skills in the theory and practice of nursing; and
4. Demonstrate professionalism and values in all responsibilities in the practice of nurse education.

## Mode of Instruction

Courses are delivered on a semestral basis using various technologies where appropriate. Virtual classrooms run in MyPortal, the learning management system of UPOU. The program follows a blended mode — combination of online and face-to-face delivery, offered jointly with UP Manila.

## Curriculum

The program is a non-thesis track with 30 units of coursework. Students must complete a capstone project, compile a professional portfolio, and pass a comprehensive examination to graduate.
