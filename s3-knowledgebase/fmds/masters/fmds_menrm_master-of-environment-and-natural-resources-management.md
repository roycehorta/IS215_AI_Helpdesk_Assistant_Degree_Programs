# Master of Environment and Natural Resources Management

## Program Description

The Master of Environment and Natural Resources Management (MENRM) Program is designed to give professionals in environment-related activities a solid foundation in the art and science of environment and natural resources management.

The MENRM Program is a single, completely ladderized post-baccalaureate degree program. Students will have the option to earn the Diploma degree (DENRM) if they exit after completing 24 units of specified courses. The Master's degree is awarded upon completing 36 units with a GWA of 2.0 or better.

Students select from two program tracks — Upland Resources Management (URM) or Coastal Resources Management (CRM) — upon completion of two core courses.

## Program Goals

The program aims to:

- Equip students with a multidisciplinary perspective in dealing with environmental issues by providing solid grounding in both natural sciences and social sciences as applied to environmental management;
- Enable students to become highly skilled and knowledgeable practitioners in either upland or coastal resources management; and
- Equip students with research skills that will allow them to contribute to the advancement of either upland or coastal resources management.

## Mode of Instruction

Courses are delivered on a semestral basis using various technologies where appropriate. Virtual classrooms run in MyPortal, the learning management system of UPOU.

## Curriculum

The MENRM program requires 36 units: 6 units of core courses, 12 units of major courses, 9 units of elective courses, 6 units of research courses, and a 3-unit Special Problem.
