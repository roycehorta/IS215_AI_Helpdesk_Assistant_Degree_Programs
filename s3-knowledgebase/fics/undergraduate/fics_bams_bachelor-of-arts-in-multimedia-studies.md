**Source:** https://fics.upou.edu.ph/bachelor-of-arts-in-multimedia-studies/

## Program Description

The **Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies (BAMS)** is an innovative and dynamic undergraduate degree offered by the University of the Philippines Open University — the first of its kind in the university. The program was specifically designed to respond to the increasing demand for skilled professionals in the rapidly evolving field of multimedia. With a fully online learning format, it aims to equip students with a comprehensive set of multimedia skills and competencies to meet contemporary challenges in the digital world.

The program's inception was driven by the growing significance of information as a valuable resource and the need for individuals who can effectively manage and utilize it across various media platforms. With the global trend highlighting the immense value of multimedia in communication, entertainment, education, and marketing, BAMS serves as a response to the industry's demand for versatile multimedia experts.

The program produces graduates who possess not only technical expertise but also a deep understanding of the philosophical and theoretical underpinnings of multimedia developments. Students explore the social implications of multimedia in today's interconnected society, gaining insight into how their work impacts diverse communities and cultures. They acquire proficiency in hardware operation and software development, and are encouraged to produce engaging multimedia knowledge products that demonstrate both artistic talent and technical skill.

Moreover, the program fosters a culture of research and development, empowering students to contribute to the ever-evolving world of multimedia. By immersing students in cutting-edge multimedia technologies and industry best practices, BAMS prepares graduates to meet the challenges and opportunities presented by emerging trends, protocols, and procedures.

---

## Program Goals

The BAMS program aims to develop among students the ability to:

1. Exhibit a deep understanding of the philosophical, theoretical, and social foundations of multimedia as a field of study and practice.
2. Demonstrate proficiency in hardware operation, software development, and applications use across various multimedia platforms.
3. Produce multimedia knowledge products that reflect both creativity and technical competence.
4. Contribute to research and development in the multimedia industry.
5. Apply knowledge of emerging multimedia technologies and industry best practices to remain relevant and adaptable in a fast-paced digital landscape.
6. Understand and analyze the social implications of multimedia in diverse communities and cultures.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught **online** following the principles of **guided independent study** and **collaborative inquiry**, consistent with UPOU's open and distance learning framework.
