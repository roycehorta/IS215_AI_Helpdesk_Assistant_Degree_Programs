## Program Description

The **Associate of Arts in Digital Design and Arts (AADDA)** is a program that provides students with foundational knowledge and practical skills in the field of digital design and art. It is designed to equip students with a comprehensive understanding of digital design principles and techniques, as well as artistic concepts and practices. It combines elements of visual art, graphic design, multimedia, and technology to prepare students for a variety of creative and technical roles in the digital media industry.

The AADDA is launched to nurture diverse creative talents. The curriculum offers a range of courses designed to cultivate both artistic and entrepreneurial abilities, ensuring that learners possess the necessary skills to succeed and navigate any economic climate. The program provides portfolio reviews, mentorship, and internship assistance — all aimed at enhancing creative capabilities, fostering professional skills, and instilling an entrepreneurial mindset that enables graduates to lead inclusively and contribute to community development.

Throughout the program, students engage in hands-on projects and assignments that simulate real-world design scenarios, such as creating digital illustrations, designing websites, developing animations, and designing user interfaces for interactive experiences. These practical experiences allow students to build a portfolio that showcases their abilities to potential employers or for further educational pursuits.

Upon completion, graduates can pursue entry-level positions in industries such as advertising, marketing, web design, multimedia production, animation studios, and game development. They may work as graphic designers, digital artists, web designers, multimedia specialists, or production assistants, among other roles. The program can also serve as a stepping stone for further education — graduates may transfer credits toward a bachelor's degree in multimedia studies, digital design, visual arts, or a related field.

---

## Program Goals

The AADDA program aims to develop among students the ability to:

1. Exhibit knowledge and skills that are mainly theoretical and/or abstract with significant depth in some areas together with wide-ranging, specialized technical, creative, and conceptual skills.
2. Perform work activities demonstrating breadth, depth, and complexity in planning and initiating alternative approaches to skills and knowledge applications across a broad range of technical and/or creative requirements.
3. Demonstrate personal and professional ethical responsibility and inclusive citizenship.
4. Demonstrate effective communication skills.
5. Integrate different knowledge domains to form multifaceted interpretations of social and cultural systems.
6. Evaluate information in the context of digital or networked problem domains.
7. Generate creative and appropriate solutions through effective and respectful collaboration with diverse groups.

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught **online** following the principles of **guided independent study** and **collaborative inquiry**, consistent with UPOU's open and distance learning framework.

The AADDA degree requires the successful completion of **72 units** in total:

| Elective GE Courses | 9 units |
| UPOU Common Courses | 12 units |
| Mandated Course (PI 100) | 3 units |
| Required Concentration Courses | 18 units |
| Elective Concentration Courses | 9 units |
| PE Courses | (8) units |
| NSTP Courses | (6) units |

> Students can tailor their elective concentration courses based on their interest area. For **video production**: MMS 173, MMS 175, and MMS 181. For **web-related work**: MMS 101, MMS 150, and MMS 151; or MMS 151, MMS 175, and MMS 176. For **creative and artistic production**: HUM 40 and any two of MMS 173, MMS 175, or MMS 176.
