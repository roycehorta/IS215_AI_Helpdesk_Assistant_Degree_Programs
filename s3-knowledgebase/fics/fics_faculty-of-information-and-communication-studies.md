# Faculty of Information and Communication Studies

## Faculty Description

The Faculty of Information and Communication Studies (FICS) is one of the three faculties of studies of the University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU). Since its inception in 2004, FICS has been committed to pioneering innovative programs, conducting impactful research, and providing essential community services.

FICS believes that technology and communication play an integral role in shaping the social and economic development of the region. It is dedicated to exploring the social impact of information and communication technology and piloting innovative applications of these technologies to meet the needs of the development assistance sector.

FICS is a degree-granting unit of the UP Open University. The faculty is comprised of 19 full-time and 31 affiliate and adjunct faculty members, all committed to fostering a culture of academic excellence, research, and public service.

## Faculty Goals

### Mission

To serve as a leading institution dedicated to advancing the field of information and communication studies in Southeast Asia, through innovative programs, impactful research, and essential community services.

### Commitment

To explore the social impact of information and communication technology and pilot innovative applications of these technologies to meet the needs of the development assistance sector.

### Motto

- Pioneer innovative programs in information and communication studies
- Conduct impactful research that advances the field
- Provide essential community services that leverage technology for social good

## Mode of Instruction

Online (Distance Education). All FICS programs are delivered entirely online, ensuring that students can access high-quality education regardless of their location or schedule.

## Courses

FICS courses are organized by program and delivered online. Each program has its own set of core, major, and elective courses. Refer to individual program pages for full course listings and descriptions.

**Thematic areas covered across FICS programs:**
- Digital Design and Arts (graphic design, digital imaging, web design, animation, multimedia production)
- Information Technology (programming, database management, networking, cybersecurity, software development)
- Multimedia Studies (multimedia production, web design, digital storytelling, user experience design)
- Computer Science (programming languages, algorithms, data structures, software engineering)
- Information Systems (systems management, database management, business intelligence, IT project management)
- Development Communication (communication theories, development theories, advocacy, social marketing)
- Communication Research (communication theory, research methodologies, media studies, organizational communication)
