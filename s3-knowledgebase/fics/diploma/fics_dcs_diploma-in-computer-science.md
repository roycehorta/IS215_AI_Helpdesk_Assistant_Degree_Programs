# Diploma in Computer Science

**Institution:** University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU)  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Information and Communication Studies  
**Level:** Graduate (Diploma)  
**Source:** https://fics.upou.edu.ph/diploma-in-computer-science/

---

## Program Description

The **Diploma in Computer Science (DCS)** program is a comprehensive and hands-on educational pathway that prepares students for a rewarding career in the dynamic field of computer science. It is designed to provide students with the knowledge and skills needed to excel in a rapidly evolving industry, emphasizing practical application through hands-on projects, coding exercises, and lab work.

The program addresses the need for personnel working in the information technology field to have an academic degree in computer science. It targets corporate information officers, EDP personnel, programmers, software developers, and teachers with minimal training in the field.

---

## Program Goals

The program aims to develop among students:

1. A solid foundation in core areas of computer science, including programming languages, data structures, algorithms, database management, software development, computer networks, and cybersecurity.
2. Problem-solving abilities and critical thinking skills necessary for success in the IT field.
3. The necessary background in computer science in preparation for advanced studies.
4. Knowledge and skills in computer science as applied to their specific fields of work.
5. Improved techniques in the practice of proper design, implementation, and maintenance of application software systems that handle large amounts of data.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are delivered **entirely online** through **MyPortal**, UPOU’s Learning Management System. The program follows the principles of distance education, allowing for flexible access to high-quality instruction regardless of location.

---

## Curriculum

The DCS Curriculum consists of **27 units** total, comprising 24 units of core courses and 3 units of elective courses. The program is designed to be completed in **four semesters** (two years).

| Course Category | Units |
|---|---|
| Core Courses | 24 units |
| Elective Course | 3 units |
| **Total** | **27 units** |
