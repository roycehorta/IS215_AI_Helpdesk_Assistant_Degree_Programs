# Faculty of Education (FED)

**Institution:** University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU)  
**Level:** Undergraduate, Graduate, and Professional  
**Source:** https://fed.upou.edu.ph/

---

## Program Description

The **Faculty of Education (FED)** is one of the primary degree-granting units of the University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU). It is mandated to offer degree programs in teacher training and to design and deliver innovative programs that reflect the expertise of UPOU while remaining responsive to evolving teacher education needs.

The Faculty adheres to the philosophy of open learning and distance education, aiming to reach a wide spectrum of learners through dynamic and alternative learning environments.

---

## Program Goals

The Faculty of Education aims to:

1. Maintain a system of continuing education to sustain professional growth among educators.
2. Promote lifelong learning through both degree and non-degree programs.
3. Develop instructional packages specifically designed for the needs of distance learners.
4. Provide high-quality teacher training that is accessible through modern technologies and opportunities.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All programs under the Faculty of Education are taught via **Distance Education (DE)**. This involves:
* **Online Learning:** Utilizing UPOU's MyPortal for course delivery and interaction.
* **Guided Independent Study:** Principles that allow students to manage their own learning pace with faculty guidance.
* **Collaborative Inquiry:** Encouraging student interaction through asynchronous discussion boards and digital tools.

| Level | Programs |

## Courses and Program of Study

The Faculty's programs are organized into the following areas of study:

### 1. Professional Teacher Training
Focuses on preparing non-education graduates for the teaching profession and upgrading the skills of current teachers through the **Professional Teaching Certification (PTC)** and **Diploma** programs.

### 2. Distance Education and Technology
Specialized programs like the **Master of Distance Education (MDE)** and the **Associate of Science in Instructional Design and Technology (ASIDT)**, which focus on the design and management of e-learning systems.

### 3. Subject Matter Specialization
Advanced studies in specific disciplines, including **Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, and Language Education** at the Diploma, Masters, and Doctorate levels.

### 4. Educational Research and Policy
The **Bachelor of Education Studies (BES)** and **PhD in Education** programs emphasize the systematic study of education policy, research literacy, and the challenges of modern educational systems.
