**Institution:** University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU)  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Education  
**Level:** Graduate Certificate (Post-Baccalaureate)  
**Source:** https://fed.upou.edu.ph/graduate-certificate/graduate-certificate-in-distance-education/

---

## Program Description

The **Graduate Certificate in Distance Education (GCDE)** is a focused, compact program offered conjointly with the **Master of Distance Education (MDE)** program. It is intended for professionals who are looking for a formal introduction to the field of distance education and graduate-level training in developing and managing distance education and e-learning programs, but who do not require a Master's degree in this field.

Students admitted to the MDE program may also opt to exit the MDE and be awarded the GCDE, provided they have completed the GCDE course requirements.

---

## Program Goals

GCDE graduates are expected to be able to:

1. Participate in the development and delivery of distance education programs that are underpinned by effective pedagogy and appropriate and sustainable use of technology.
2. Participate in the management of distance education programs in different contexts — including educational, business, and non-profit organizations — to meet the needs of target learners.
3. Use their knowledge of theories, models, and principles of open learning and distance education to critically examine various aspects of DE programs from a variety of perspectives.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught **online** following the principles of **guided independent study** and **collaborative inquiry**, consistent with UPOU's open and distance learning framework.

### Graduation and Transition to MDE

A GCDE graduate who wishes to apply for admission or re-admission to the MDE program must have a **General Weighted Average (GWA) of 2.0 or better** in the GCDE.

Upon admission to the MDE program, courses completed for the GCDE may be credited to the MDE subject to the following conditions:

1. The course to be credited must have been taken **within three years** from the application for crediting.
2. The student must have earned a **grade of 2.0 or better** in the course to be credited.

If a course does not meet one of these two conditions, it may still be credited provided the student takes and passes a **validation exam** for that course before or within the semester of admission to the MDE program.

Up to **18 units** of courses completed for the GCDE may be credited to the MDE, subject to the foregoing conditions and other relevant University policies.
