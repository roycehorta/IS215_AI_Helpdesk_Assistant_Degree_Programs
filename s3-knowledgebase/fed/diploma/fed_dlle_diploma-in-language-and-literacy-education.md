## Program Description

The **Diploma in Language and Literacy Education (DLLE)** is a ladderized program designed to upgrade and update language and literacy professionals, including teachers, trainers, administrators, curriculum developers, and researchers. It provides a strong foundation in the field by integrating knowledge from cognitive psychology, linguistics, and cultural studies.

---

## Program Goals

The DLLE program aims to develop qualified professionals who are able to:

1. Understand the theoretical and evidence-based foundations of language and literacy processes and instruction.
2. Design and develop theoretically grounded and research-based materials, instructional approaches, and curricula.
3. Organize and implement integrated, balanced, and comprehensive language and literacy programs.
4. Assess and evaluate processes and outcomes of language and literacy instruction through various tools and practices.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught **online** following the principles of **guided independent study** and **collaborative inquiry**.

---

## Curriculum

The program consists of **21 units** of core coursework. Students who complete the Diploma may apply for the ladderized Master of Arts in Language and Literacy Education (MALLE) program.
