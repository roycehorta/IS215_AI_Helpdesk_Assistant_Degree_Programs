## Program Description

The **Diploma in Social Studies Education (DSSE)** aims to develop practitioners and leaders who will strengthen Social Studies education. The program is ladderized with the Master of Arts in Social Studies Education (MASSE).

---

## Program Goals

The program aims to develop social studies education practitioners who can:

1. Demonstrate a deep understanding of the theoretical and disciplinal foundations of social studies education.
2. Design and develop theoretically grounded and research-based curricula and instructional materials.
3. Organize and implement relevant, interdisciplinary, and coherent social studies programs.
4. Assess and evaluate processes and outcomes of social studies instruction.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught **online** following the principles of **guided independent study** and **collaborative inquiry**.
