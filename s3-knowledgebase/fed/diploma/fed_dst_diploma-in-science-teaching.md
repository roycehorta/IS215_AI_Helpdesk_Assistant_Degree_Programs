# Diploma in Science Teaching

**Institution:** University of the Philippines Open University (UPOU)  
**Faculty:** Faculty of Education

**Source:** https://fed.upou.edu.ph/diploma/diploma-in-science-teaching/

---

## Program Description

The **Diploma in Science Teaching (DST)** is a post-baccalaureate degree program designed to address the need for teachers to develop the breadth and depth of content knowledge in science courses, namely biology, chemistry, and physics.

---

## Program Goals

The program seeks to:

1. Develop a strong foundation in the student's chosen area of science and encourage lifelong interest in this area.
2. Develop a critical and research-based understanding of science education frameworks, models, and approaches.
3. Develop the skills necessary to teach science effectively, including appropriate use of learning technologies.
4. Promote habits of critical reflection on teaching and learning, and encourage active participation in communities of practice.
5. Develop an appreciation of the cultural significance of science and its relation to social conditions.

---

## Mode of Instruction

All courses are taught **online** following the principles of **guided independent study** and **collaborative inquiry**.

---

## Curriculum

The DST program consists of **24 units** of coursework. This is composed of 9 units of core courses and 15 units of major courses in a chosen area of specialization (Biology, Chemistry, or Physics).

| Course Category | Units |
|---|---|
| Core Courses | 9 units |
| Major Courses | 15 units |
| **Total** | **24 units** |
